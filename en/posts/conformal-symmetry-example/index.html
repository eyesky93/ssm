An example post in Physics → Conformal Field Theory, about symmetry and scale.

This example post belongs to Conformal Field Theory, a subtopic of Physics.

Symmetry and scale

A scale transformation enlarges or shrinks distances by the same factor. Conformal transformations preserve angles locally, while allowing the scale factor to vary from point to point.

These symmetries strongly constrain correlation functions in conformal field theory. They provide a starting point for studying how observables depend on the separation between points.
