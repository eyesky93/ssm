A repeated phrase is never heard in exactly the same context twice. What sounded like an opening can return as a memory, a question, or a conclusion.

Repetition and change

The interest often lies in the small displacement: a changed bass note, a delayed entrance, a silence where the ear expected an answer. Structure becomes audible through difference.
