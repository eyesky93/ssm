One notebook, two languages

The English and Hebrew editions share a structure, but each post can exist in one language or in both. A translation is never created or displayed unless you write it.

Mathematics stays readable

Inline mathematics such as E=mc2E = mc^2 remains part of the sentence, while displayed equations receive their own space:

𝒜(s,t)=∑n=0∞an(t)sn.\mathcal{A}(s,t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n(t)s^n.

The source for every post remains a normal LaTeX or LyX document.
